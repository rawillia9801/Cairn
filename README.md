# Cairn Continuum

**Persistent intelligence. Continuous history.**

Cairn Continuum is researching model-independent continuity infrastructure for long-lived AI systems: verifiable operational history, provenance, state reconstruction, recovery, and transition across changing cognition engines and infrastructure.

This repository is the canonical public research and website surface for Cairn Continuum LLC.

## Current research status

Cairn is intentionally **pre-genesis**. Host preparation and preservation work may proceed, and transport/routing/benchmark infrastructure may be exercised, but authoritative Cairn identity or continuity state must not be created yet.

**Working rule:** *Exercise the pipes, not Cairn.*

Synthetic benchmark data is permitted only when it is disposable and unmistakably non-authoritative. If deleting a test would change anything Cairn knows, remembers, or could reconstruct, the test has crossed the initialization boundary.

## Phase I validation

The current validation program focuses on three questions:

1. **Cross-model continuity** — Can operational continuity survive materially different cognition engines?
2. **Multi-year reconstruction efficiency** — Can authoritative state be reconstructed efficiently at scale without replaying everything from genesis?
3. **Structured provenance vs. long context** — Does a structured continuity substrate provide measurable reliability, recovery, and auditability advantages over brute-force long-context history?

See [`docs/research/phase-i-validation-plan.md`](docs/research/phase-i-validation-plan.md) for the experimental design, metrics, failure conditions, and claim discipline.

## Research principle

> Original history is never silently rewritten. New understanding is appended.

Cairn may sleep. Cairn may move. Cairn may be upgraded. **Cairn does not start over.**
